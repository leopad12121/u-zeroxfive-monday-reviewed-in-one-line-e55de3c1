# Candidates — Monday, Reviewed in One Line

Judge criteria (from the kit, most important first):
1. Is a one-line review of the entrant's own Monday rather than a joke about something else.
2. Gets the bigger laugh, the line a reader would laugh at rather than merely nod at.
3. Names a concrete detail of the Monday, a meeting, a commute, an inbox, over generic tiredness.
4. Lands the joke inside the line with no wasted words, so the turn arrives before the reader gives up.
5. Is the line a reader would repeat to a coworker the next morning.

Scored out of 25 (5 per criterion).

## 1. Would not recommend — 22/25
> Monday review: one star. My alarm went off, my laptop updated, and my 9 a.m. standup started without me. Would not recommend.
Concrete (alarm, laptop update, standup) and the review-voice closer is repeatable. The three-item list is a beat long, and "alarm went off" is the weakest detail.

## 2. Triple-digit inbox — 20/25
> My Monday got a one-star review from me: the coffee machine broke, the standup ran long, and my inbox hit triple digits before lunch.
Concrete, but "triple digits" is vague rather than a number, and three mild misfortunes add up to a nod more than a laugh.

## 3. Restraining order — 23/25
> Monday, reviewed: the commute took an hour, the meeting could have been an email, and my inbox filed a restraining order against me.
Three concrete Monday details, and the inbox filing a restraining order is a specific, surprising turn that earns the laugh and is easy to repeat to a coworker.

## 4. Meeting about a meeting — 19/25
> I rate my Monday one star: I spent 40 minutes in a meeting about a meeting, then my laptop restarted to install updates.
"Meeting about a meeting" is a stock line every office worker has heard, so the laugh is borrowed; the laptop restart is fine but mild.

## 5. Two stars — 21/25
> Monday review: two stars, one for the coffee, one for surviving. The 9 a.m. standup lasted 47 minutes and decided nothing.
Charming opener and a concrete, funny detail (47 minutes, decided nothing). Loses a point on criterion 4: the two-sentence setup delays the turn.

## 6. 214 unread — 20/25
> My Monday earned one star: my alarm snoozed itself, the train was late, and my inbox greeted me with 214 unread emails.
"Alarm snoozed itself" is a good turn and 214 is nicely specific, but the list of three grievances reads like a complaint, not a review with a punchline.

## 7. Cold coffee — 21/25
> Monday: one star. The meeting that could have been an email ran 50 minutes, and my coffee went cold during the part that mattered.
"Coffee went cold during the part that mattered" is a lovely, repeatable turn. The meeting-could-have-been-an-email opener is stock, which costs originality.

## 8. Granola bar lunch — 22/25
> Reviewing my Monday: the Wi-Fi died mid-standup, my calendar triple-booked itself, and lunch was a granola bar eaten over the keyboard.
Very concrete and vivid — the granola bar over the keyboard is the kind of detail a coworker repeats. Slightly list-like; the laugh builds rather than lands.

## Pick
Candidate 3, 23/25 — the strongest on criteria 2, 3 and 5: three concrete Monday details, a surprising specific punchline (the inbox filing a restraining order), and a line short enough to repeat at the coffee machine.

## Revision
